Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-29
  Features:
    - Vanilla turret buffs (K2-style, buffs not nerfs): gun-turret range 18 -> 24 and damage_modifier 1 -> 2 (48 per uranium round, 480 DPS); laser-turret range 24 -> 28 and damage_modifier 2 -> 4 (40 per shot, 60 DPS). Damage buffs are per-shot, not fire rate, so the same kills cost half the bullet belt. Both controlled by startup settings (on by default). heavy-gun-turret is untouched - it stays the DPS wall on Vulcanus.
    - Stack test is now A/B: Phase A asserts vanilla base values without the mod, Phase B asserts the roster, parent prototypes and the buffed values.

Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-10-29
  Features:
    - Parent set updated to the installed stack: Missile Defense Systems Redux (fork of missile_defense_systems_sa) and doeWorks Rocket Artillery Continued (fork of doeworks-deer); Two more Turrets, Heavy Gun Turret and VortiK Cannon Turret unchanged. Roster notes now record that Two more Turrets defers to the standalone parents when they are present, and that MDS Redux keeps the vanilla rocket turret (the original _sa mod removed it).
    - Stack-load test (make test-stack): loads the live save-synced mod set plus this mod through --scenario2map and asserts the roster, the parents' turret prototypes, Two more Turrets' deferral, and the vanilla rocket turret's survival.
